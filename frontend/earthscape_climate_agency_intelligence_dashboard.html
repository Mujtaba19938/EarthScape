EarthScape Big Data Architecture Overview

The EarthScape platform ingests heterogeneous streams from satellites and meteorological stations through Kafka brokers into HDFS. Batch aggregation runs via Hadoop MapReduce, while real-time anomaly detection is powered by PyTorch models served over FastAPI endpoints.
