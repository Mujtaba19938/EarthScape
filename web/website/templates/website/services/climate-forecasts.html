About Service

About the climate forecasts service

Climate Forecasts uses machine learning to predict temperature, flag unusual air quality and measure how climate variables move together.

01

Predict the Trend

Several methods compete on nine NASA GISTEMP series: linear trends over recent windows and Holt exponential smoothing.

02

Test It Fairly

The chosen method is scored once on the final 10 years, 2016 to 2025, against simply repeating the last value.

03

Retrain on New Data

An administrator can press Retrain models at any time. Each run is saved with its scores, so versions can be compared.
