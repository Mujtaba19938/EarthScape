About the climate analytics service

Climate Analytics shows what the records say. Every chart comes from weather station and satellite-derived data processed with Apache Hadoop.

01

Choose a Region

Pick a latitude zone, a variable and a time span, and the charts update to match.

02

Compare the Records

Follow regional temperature and rainfall by month, alongside NASA temperature anomalies and Mauna Loa CO₂.

03

Check the Quality

A data quality table shows how many readings were valid, missing, failed quality checks or were malformed.

How We Work

Honest numbers you can check

Results are worked out ahead of time by five MapReduce jobs and saved to MongoDB, so a chart loads without waiting for Hadoop.

Station readings more than 3 standard deviations from the mean for that station and month are flagged. The baseline covers only 2020 and 2023, so the dashboard labels it as short.
