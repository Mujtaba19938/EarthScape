About the reports and export service

Reports & Export gets the data to where it’s needed. Signed-in users can download results from the Reports & Export page.

01

Choose the Data

Pick a report, such as temperature anomalies, monthly station means or air quality.

02

Download CSV for spreadsheets, JSON for software, or GeoJSON for mapping tools.

03

Take It Further

CSV files are also prepared for Tableau, ready for a workbook to be built on top of them.

Honest numbers you can check

Dates use the ISO 8601 standard and map data uses GeoJSON, so exports open cleanly in other tools.

Every value in a report comes from the same pipeline as the dashboard, so the numbers match what users see on screen.
