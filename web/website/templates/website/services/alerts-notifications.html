About Service

About the alerts and notifications service

Alerts & Notifications watches the live readings for you. Administrators set the rules, and everyone signed in can see what has been triggered.

01

Set a Rule

An administrator chooses a measurement, a limit and a severity on the Alert Management page.

02

Check Every Reading

Each collection of live data is checked against the rules, so an alert appears within about 15 minutes.

03

Follow It Through

An alert opens when a rule is broken and closes itself when the readings recover.
