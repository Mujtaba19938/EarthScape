Our Mission

Our mission is to turn climate data into decisions

Climate records are large, scattered and hard to use. EarthScape stores them in one place with Apache Hadoop, processes them, and presents the results on clear dashboards.

We add live readings, machine learning forecasts and automatic alerts, so people can see what’s changing and act on it. Every forecast is scored against a simple baseline, and every limit is stated on screen.
