Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the climate data come from?

From public sources: NOAA GHCN-Daily station records, NASA GISTEMP, NOAA Mauna Loa CO₂, NASA POWER, OpenAQ and live readings from Open-Meteo.

How often is the live data updated?

New readings for 12 cities arrive every 15 minutes, and alert rules are checked after each one. Readings are saved to HDFS in hourly batches.

Can I trust the forecasts?

Each forecast is tested on 2016 to 2025 against simply repeating the last value. It beat that baseline on 5 of 9 series, and the dashboard shows the score for each one.

Does EarthScape process satellite imagery?

Not yet. It uses satellite-derived values from NASA POWER as tables. Image files can be uploaded and stored, but they aren’t processed.

Who can sign in to the dashboard?

Administrators and analysts. The server checks each person’s role on every request, so only administrators can change rules, upload data or retrain models.

Can I export the data?

Yes. Signed-in users can export results as CSV, JSON or GeoJSON from the Reports & Export page.
